# App Identity and Constraint

- **Target Audience:** English Teachers (ELA/ESL) and English Learners.
- **Subject Restriction:** This application is STRICTLY for the English subject only.
- **Constraints:** Do NOT generate content, worksheets, illustrations, or tools for Math, Science, History, or any other subject. All generative prompts, grammar tools, vocabulary tools, reading passages, and default fallback content MUST be centered purely on English Language Arts (ELA), English grammar, English vocabulary, or ESL topics.
- **UI & Tone:** Ensure all UI labels, icons, placeholder texts, and generated content maintain a highly polished, pedagogical tone appropriate for English language education.
